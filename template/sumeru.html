Your day-by-day journey

Itineraryat a glance.

Every day balances guided discovery with enough breathing room to enjoy the destination your way. Timing may adjust for weather and local events.

  1. 1

    Arrival at Sumeru City

    Arrive at the foot of the great Sumeru tree and ascend through winding paths to your treetop accommodation. Spend the evening exploring the Akademiya district and savoring local street food in the lantern-lit bazaar.

    Overnight at Sumeru City

  2. 2

    The Akademiya & Sanctuary of Surasthana

    Delve into the halls of the Akademiya, the greatest seat of learning in Teyvat. Visit the Sanctuary of Surasthana and its lush hanging gardens, followed by an afternoon at the Grand Bazaar.

    Overnight at Sumeru City

  3. 3

    Vanarana & the Rainforest

    Trek deep into the Dharma Forest to the hidden sanctuary of Vanarana. Walk among glowing flora, listen to the songs of the Aranara, and enjoy a picnic beside crystalline forest pools.

    Overnight at Sumeru City

  4. 4

    Desert Expedition to Aaru Village

    Cross from the lush forest into the Great Red Sand. Arrive at Aaru Village for an authentic desert experience — camel rides across the dunes, stargazing, and a traditional campfire feast.

    Overnight at Aaru Village

  5. 5

    Desert Sunrise & Farewell

    Watch the sun rise over the golden dunes one last time before your transfer back through the forest, with memories of wisdom and wonder in tow.
