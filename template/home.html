About our company

Seven nations.One trusted agency.

WaypointGO is a Teyvat travel agency that offers safe, structured commercial tours across all seven nations: Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Natlan, Fontaine, and Snezhnaya. Each region has its own climate, customs, entry protocols, and local logistics, and we have mapped them all so you never have to navigate them alone. We turn firsthand route knowledge, regional delicacies, and cultural heritage into curated expeditions that regular civilians, scholars, and merchants can join with confidence.

Every expedition includes regional transport, hand-picked stays, selected meals, and passes to the listed attractions, led by a local guide with gentle pacing and 24/7 support. Our team stays beside you from the moment you send an inquiry until you are safely back home, so every step of the trip feels organized and worry-free. Whether you are a first-time traveler or a seasoned wanderer, our team is here to answer your questions, tailor your itinerary, and make sure each journey leaves you with a story worth telling.

Our Promise Safe journeys. Structured tours. Every nation of Teyvat.

Meet the visionary director

A traveler once.A wayfinder
always.

Originated from the extensive travels and firsthand experiences of a retired Traveler who journeyed across every corner of Teyvat. Over years of exploration, the founder developed unmatched familiarity with the distinct environments, local lore, culinary delicacies, and regional pathways across all seven territories.

Recognizing that regular civilians, scholars, and merchants lacked safe, structured access to experience these distant nations, the founder established WaypointGO as a dedicated travel agency to transform years of personal adventuring insights into curated, accessible commercial tours.

What is included in the tour price?

Every expedition covers regional transport, hand-picked stays, selected meals, and passes to the listed attractions. Personal shopping and optional side trips are not included.

Can I customize a regional itinerary?

Yes. Tell Katheryne which stops you want to add, swap, or skip in the form below, and her team will draft a custom route and an updated quote within two days.

Do I need visa to enter Teyvat?

No visa is required for standard guided journeys. Katheryne's team provides all regional entry permits and route documentation before departure.

Are these tours suitable for first time travelers?

Absolutely. Every journey is led by a local guide, with gentle pacing and 24/7 support, so first-time travelers can explore each nation without worrying about the details.
