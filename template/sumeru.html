Your day-by-day journey

Itineraryat a glance.

Every day balances guided discovery with enough breathing room to enjoy the destination your way. Timing may adjust for weather and local events.

  1. 1

    Arrival & Sumeru City Exploration

    Begin with a guided arrival and private hotel transfer, then head into the heart of Sumeru City. Take part in a Darshan guided seminar tour and explore the House of Daena library, followed by a local spice tasting at the Grand Bazaar. Wind down in the evening with a regional dinner featuring Sabz Meat Stew.

    Overnight at Wikala Funduq

  2. 2

    Gandharva Ville Eco-Retreat

    Make your way to Gandharva Ville in the morning for a full day of rainforest exploration. Enjoy an Avidya Forest canopy trek, master Four-Leaf Sigil grappling, and participate in a botanical herbalism workshop with the forest rangers. Return to the city in the evening for dinner at leisure.

    Overnight at Wikala Funduq

  3. 3

    Caravan Ribat Gateway & Departure

    Travel south to the fortified border post of Caravan Ribat for a desert preparation briefing and a short Sumpter Beast pack-caravan ride along the canyon paths. Spend the afternoon picking up local souvenirs and enjoying a farewell oasis lunch before your private departure transfer home.

    Overnight at Wikala Funduq
