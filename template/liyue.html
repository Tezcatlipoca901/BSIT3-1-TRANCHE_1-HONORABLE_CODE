Your day-by-day journey

Itineraryat a glance.

Every day balances guided discovery with enough breathing room to enjoy the destination your way. Timing may adjust for weather and local events.

  1. 1

    Explore Mt. Aocang

    Begin your journey with a morning ascent through terraced paths and stone steps, reaching viewpoints that reveal the full scope of Liyue's landscape. Return in the afternoon for rest and exploration of local markets.

    Overnight at Liyue Harbor

  2. 2

    The Galloway by Liyue

    Spend the day discovering the harbor's historic districts, visiting the Yujing Terrace and witnessing the craftsmanship of local artisans. Evening brings a traditional tea ceremony and harbor-side dining.

    Overnight at Liyue Harbor

  3. 3

    Ascending the Peaks of the Adepti

    Journey into the mountain wilderness with a guided trek through ancient trails. Visit sacred sites and hear tales of the Adepti before returning to the harbor for a farewell dinner.

    Overnight at Liyue Harbor

  4. 4

    The Road of Prosperity

    Your final morning includes a visit to Wangshu Inn, where you'll enjoy panoramic views and signature dishes before your departure transfer with memories of stone and commerce.
