Your day-by-day journey

Itineraryat a glance.

Every day balances guided discovery with enough breathing room to enjoy the destination your way. Timing may adjust for weather and local events.

  1. 1

    Arrival at Romaritime Harbor

    Arrive by steamship at the scenic Romaritime Harbor and take in the elegant waterfront. Check in to your canal-side accommodation and enjoy an evening stroll through gas-lit boulevards, sampling Fontainian pastries.

    Overnight at Court of Fontaine

  2. 2

    Court of Fontaine & Opera Epiclese

    Explore the grand courthouse and attend a matinee at the Opera Epiclese. Witness the famous Fontaine trial system and wander through the galleries displaying masterworks of Fontainian art.

    Overnight at Court of Fontaine

  3. 3

    Erinnyes Forest & Waterfalls

    Journey into the misty Erinnyes Forest for a guided nature walk past cascading waterfalls, ancient ruins, and crystalline pools. Return for an evening of fine Fontainian dining.

    Overnight at Court of Fontaine

  4. 4

    Underwater Exploration

    Descend beneath Fontaine's waters for a guided tour of the subaquatic landscapes, discovering the unique underwater ecosystems and architecture that make Fontaine truly one of a kind.

    Overnight at Court of Fontaine

  5. 5

    Canal Cruise & Farewell

    Enjoy a final morning canal cruise through the city's waterways, passing beneath ornate bridges and along flower-lined banks before your departure transfer.
